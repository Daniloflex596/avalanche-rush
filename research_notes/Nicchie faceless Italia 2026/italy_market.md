# Italian social/short-video market and best niches 2025-2026 (as of 2026-10-07)

## 1. User numbers, demographics and time spent per platform

### Takeaway
Italy has about 41.2M social identities (69.7% of population, shrinking slightly). YouTube (41.2M ad reach), Instagram (29.9M), Facebook (28.5M) and TikTok (22.0M 18+) are all large. TikTok and Instagram are growing, Facebook and YouTube reach are flat or declining. Population is old (median age 48.2), which shapes which niches work.

### Cited Findings
- 41.2M social media user identities in Oct 2025 (69.7% of population), down 1.0M (-2.4%) vs end 2024; 49.9% female / 50.1% male; 77.5% of internet users use social — [DataReportal Digital 2026 Italy](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-italy)
- Ad reach late 2025: YouTube 41.2M (69.7%); Instagram 29.9M (50.5%, +1.2M y/y); Facebook 28.5M (48.1%, -750K y/y); TikTok 22.0M adults 18+ (43.5% of adults, +1.41M) — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-italy)
- Gender of ad audience (F/M): TikTok 59.7/40.3 (skews female); Instagram 51.5/47.0; Facebook 49.6/50.0; YouTube 49.9/50.1; Pinterest 70.3% female; X only 29.8% female — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-italy)
- Population 59.1M, median age 48.2; 65+ = 25.1%, 55-64 = 15.8%, 45-54 = 15.0%, 35-44 = 11.7%, 25-34 = 10.7%, 18-24 = 7.2%; internet users 53.1M (89.9%) — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-italy)
- TikTok: 23.9M monthly active users in Italy (Jan-Jun 2026 average, TikTok Newsroom Italia, via Kolsquare); 52.2% women / 47.8% men; ~30 hours/month per user (~1 h/day); 8,000+ TikTok Shop sellers — [Kolsquare](https://www.kolsquare.com/it/blog/tiktok-statistiche-e-opportunita-per-linfluencer-marketing). Milano Finanza (Apr 2026) headline: 21k TikTok Shop sellers in first year, boom among over-40s (41%) — [Milano Finanza](https://www.milanofinanza.it/news/tiktok-shop-in-italia-21-mila-venditori-nel-primo-anno-di-attivita-boom-tra-gli-over-40-con-il-41-degli-202604271713084361) (snippet only, not fully read)
- Older TikTok Italy data (TikTok white paper, Istat Jan 2022): 16-24 = 43.5% of users; 80 min/day on TikTok vs ~30 on Instagram (company source, 4 years old) — [Open Influence/TikTok white paper](https://openinfluence.com/wp-content/uploads/OI_TIkTok_WhitePaper_LUG22-1.pdf)
- NapoleonCat (platform marketing-API counts): 44.5M Facebook users in Italy July 2026 (74.7% of population), largest group 25-34 (10.2M) — different method than DataReportal, higher — [NapoleonCat](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/social-media-users-in-italy/)
- 68% of Italian marketers use YouTube for influencer campaigns, 78% TikTok, 94% Instagram; 97% prefer micro-influencers; 78% plan bigger influencer budgets in 2026 — [Kolsquare](https://www.kolsquare.com/it/blog/tiktok-statistiche-e-opportunita-per-linfluencer-marketing)

### Inferences
- With 40% of the population 55+, Facebook and YouTube (broad age) are the best fits for older-skewing evergreen content (storie, curiosità, salute, cucina tradizionale); TikTok is a young, female-skewing audience.
- Facebook reach is large and older; the NapoleonCat vs DataReportal gap (44.5M vs 28.5M) means Facebook figures should be quoted with method caveat.

### Gaps
- DataReportal Italy page did not give time spent per platform or age splits per platform; no current Italian age split for TikTok/Instagram/Shorts found. No Agcom/Istat per-platform 2026 figures found. No Italy-specific Shorts viewership figures found (only global 200B daily views, [Kolsquare YouTube](https://www.kolsquare.com/it/blog/statistiche-youtube-dati-italiani-tendenze-e-strategie-per-influencer-marketing)).

## 2. Most popular topics/niches in Italy

### Takeaway
No rigorous Italy-wide ranking of niches by views was found. Indirect evidence: football dominates (even without Italy at World Cup 2026), cooking is strong on TikTok, news/entertainment pages and food accounts lead follower rankings. Treat niche popularity as indicative.

### Cited Findings
- Football: Italian audiences remain "thirsty" for football content in summer 2026 although Italy did not qualify for the World Cup; Instagram dominant for footballers, TikTok growing; FIFA chose TikTok as preferred platform for World Cup clips — [Kolsquare](https://www.kolsquare.com/it/blog/calciatori-italiani-come-influencer-durante-il-mondiale-il-paradosso-dellassenza-azzurra); [La Voce di New York](https://lavocedinewyork.com/news/2026/01/09/la-fifa-porta-il-mondiale-2026-su-tiktok-in-arrivo-dirette-e-conteuti-esclusivi/)
- Formats predicted to work for football shorts: reactions, meme clips, player comparisons, predictions, recaps, 15-30 s — [Filmora](https://filmora.wondershare.it/video-editing-tips/idee-contenuti-social-mondiali-2026.html) (marketing guide, low rigour)
- Cooking: top Italian TikTok food account ~18.4M followers (Infludata, May 2026; list has data inconsistencies) — [Infludata Food IT TikTok](https://infludata.com/en/rankings/top-20-influencer-italy-food-tiktok); a Sansepolcro creator with 3.6M TikTok followers doing traditional cooking — [La Nazione](https://www.lanazione.it/arezzo/cronaca/gli-aretini-piu-seguiti-che-sbancano-sui-social-cf4d69f2)
- News/entertainment: Heepsy Sept 2026 news list: GialloZafferano Loves Italy ~23.4M, Jess ~10.2M, Joey Nero ~6.1M — [Heepsy](https://www.heepsy.com/en/find-influencers/tiktok/news/italy)
- Art & creativity: 10,292 Italian creators, top ~2.0M, avg engagement 8.7% (many viewers outside Italy) — [Infludata Art](https://infludata.com/rankings/top-20-influencer-italy-art-tiktok)
- Tech: top Italian TikTok tech profiles only ~1.3M, 668K, 458K followers (Scrumball, vendor page) — [Scrumball](https://www.scrumball.com/ranking/top-tech-influencers-on-tiktok-in-italy). Music creators on TikTok Italy top out in low hundreds of thousands — [Heepsy](https://www.heepsy.com/top-tiktok/music/italy)
- Khabane Lame (Italy-based, wordless comedy, faces on camera) was the most-followed TikTok creator worldwide as of Mar 2026 — [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1078315/most-followed-tiktok-global) (figure redacted in snippet)

### Inferences
- Tech and music have small top-end audiences on Italian TikTok, suggesting either low demand or low supply; unclear which.
- Football and food have the clearest demand signals; both are competitive with well-known personalities.

### Gaps
- No data on gossip, true crime, finanza personale, oroscopo, film/serie, fai da te, salute view volumes in Italy. No Italian TikTok/Reels trend report for 2026 beyond the football/World Cup items. Reddit r/Italia, Ninja Marketing, Wired Italia, Social Blade and HypeAuditor Italy were not retrieved.

## 4. CPM/RPM and monetization limits in Italy

### Takeaway
Italian long-form RPM is far below US; Shorts RPM is tiny everywhere (cents). Monetization eligibility in Italy: TikTok Creator Rewards listed as available by 2026 third-party sources (not official); YouTube Shorts and Facebook availability in Italy not confirmed in sources found.

### Cited Findings
- Italy long-form YouTube RPM about 0.50-4 EUR per 1,000 views, 8-12 EUR in finance/B2B/health; Shorts RPM about 0.01-0.07 EUR per 1,000 views; Italian CPM about 2.20 EUR gross — [European Funding Guide](https://www.european-funding-guide.eu/it/quanto-si-guadagna-con-youtube) (single secondary source)
- US long-form RPM about $10.81 (Lenos case study); Shorts $0.01-0.08 generally — via [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/youtube-shorts-rpm/)
- YouTube Shorts creators get 45% of Shorts revenue pool after music licensing — [European Funding Guide](https://www.european-funding-guide.eu/it/quanto-si-guadagna-con-youtube)
- YPP: ads need 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours or 10M Shorts views in 90 days; 500-sub tier gives fan funding only; the 500-sub tier was initially US/UK/Canada/Taiwan/South Korea with later expansion — [VidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-relaxes-monetization-rules/); [Citizen Digital](https://citizen.digital/article/youtube-has-lowered-monetisation-eligibility-to-500-subscribers-n321913). Italy is a YPP country, but its inclusion in the expanded tier was not confirmed ([Google troubleshooter](https://support.google.com/youtube/troubleshooter/13536952), list truncated)
- YouTube Studio now separates "engaged views" (used for monetization) from raw views from April 2026 — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/it/trend-youtube/) (secondary)
- TikTok Creator Rewards: Italy listed as eligible by several 2026 third-party guides; requirements 18+, 10K followers, 100K views in 30 days; no official list; check in TikTok Studio — [SocialRevver](https://socialrevver.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program); [Multilogin](https://multilogin.com/blog/mobile/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/); [TT Calculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-fund-countries/)
- TikTok payout not fixed per 1,000 views; depends on country, length, engagement — [Kolsquare](https://www.kolsquare.com/it/blog/tiktok-statistiche-e-opportunita-per-linfluencer-marketing)
- Facebook Content Monetization Program merges In-Stream Ads, Ads on Reels and Performance Bonus; Italy availability not confirmed — [HTML.it](https://www.html.it/magazine/facebook-nuovo-programma-di-monetizzazione-per-i-creator/)

### Inferences
- Shorts monetization alone is near-worthless in Italy (cents per 1,000 views); revenue likely must come from long-form, affiliate, TikTok Shop, brand deals or funnels.
- High-CPM Italian niches (finanza, B2B, salute) are the monetization-favored ones.

### Gaps
- No primary YouTube/TikTok/Meta statement confirming Italy availability; no Italian TikTok or Facebook RPM figures; UK RPM not found.

## 5. Under-served Italian niches vs English

### Takeaway
No source directly measures supply gaps. Weak signals: Italian tech and music TikTok top creators are small; localized network content (123 Go!) dominates generic YouTube hacks/curiosità with poor-quality Italian; Italian faceless true crime/horror channels are in the low hundreds of thousands. Gap claims below are hypotheses to test.

### Cited Findings
- See section 2 (small Italian tech/music tops) and section 3 (poor-quality Italian localizations, small native faceless channels).
- Italian marketers favor micro-influencers (10-100K), suggesting demand for mid-sized niche accounts — [Kolsquare](https://www.kolsquare.com/it/blog/tiktok-statistiche-e-opportunita-per-linfluencer-marketing)

### Inferences
- Candidate gaps: native-quality Italian curiosità/storytelling, true crime/cold case narration, horror stories, finanza personale for Italians (high RPM), tech explainers, over-45 audience content on Facebook/YouTube.
- Risk: football and food are saturated by known personalities.
