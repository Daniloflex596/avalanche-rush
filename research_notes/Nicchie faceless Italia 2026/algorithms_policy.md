# Algorithm behaviour and content policies for faceless stock-footage + subtitle short-form pages (state as of 2026-10-07)

Source-quality warning: I could only retrieve one primary page (YouTube Help). TikTok and Meta official pages returned redirects, 404 or empty content. Most other material is third-party (vendor blogs that sell faceless tools). Items are tagged [PRIMARY] or [SECONDARY]; treat secondary numbers as unverified.

## YouTube: inauthentic content policy, 2026 changes, Shorts signals, Shorts RPM (incl. Italy)

### Takeaway
Since 15 July 2025 YouTube's monetization rule explicitly names template-made, mass-produced, low-variation content (slideshows, same-narration videos, generic AI templates) as ineligible, and it applies to Shorts and to the whole channel. Stock footage + subtitles + synthetic voice in a repeated template is exactly the pattern flagged. Shorts RPM is very low everywhere (about 0.03-0.15 USD/1000 views typical); no Italy-specific Shorts RPM was found.

### Cited Findings
- [PRIMARY] July 15, 2025 update note: policy renamed from "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" to cover repetitive or mass-produced content; inauthentic content was always ineligible; reused-content policy unchanged. The policy applies to all video including Shorts. — [YouTube Help, YPP policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- [PRIMARY] Section "Generic or repetitive content": not monetizable = content that looks template-made or feels repetitive; similar content with low educational value/minimal variation; image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with little narrative or value; AI-generated content from generic templates giving an impression of mass production; AI-stitched clips without clear narrative. Allowed = shared intros/outros if main content differs; series with distinct storylines, focus or concepts; AI tools used when the result shows creative vision (AI-assisted scripts/backgrounds). — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- [PRIMARY] Reused content is judged at channel level; allowed only with meaningful transformation (commentary, critical review, substantive editing); compilations from other platforms with little narrative not allowed. AI personas posing as experts on health/legal/financial/political topics not allowed. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- [SECONDARY] Rene Ritchie (YouTube Creator Liaison) called the July 2025 change a "minor update"; YouTube said it is not a ban on AI per se. — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-clarifies-monetisation-policy-targeting-inauthentic-content-9493155); [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/)
- [SECONDARY, single source] 2026 timeline: January 2026 enforcement wave removing many channels (approx. 35M subs, approx. $10M/yr ad revenue) with shared traits "synthetic narration, templated thumbnails, stock-footage loops, unsustainable upload pace"; enforcement described as channel-level. 27 May 2026: YouTube auto-applies AI labels on detected undisclosed photorealistic AI. — [AIR Media-Tech timeline](https://air.io/en/monetization/youtube-monetization-policy-changes-2026-a-complete-dated-timeline). The "16 channels / 4.7B views" figure is from OutlierKit and unconfirmed — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/blog/youtube-updates-july-2026) (via search summary; not fetched).
- [SECONDARY, single source, verify] YPP overhaul announced 10 Aug 2026, effective 1 Feb 2027: new applicants need 1,000 subs + 8,000 watch hours/365 days or 20M Shorts views/90 days (up from 4,000 hours / 10M Shorts views); Shorts Creator Pool payouts require 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days; activity requirement (1,000 watch hours/365d, or 1M Shorts views/90d, or 2 long videos/5 Shorts per 90 days); Premium Lite expands with 60% of net revenue to creator pool. Applications in by 31 Jan 2027 reviewed under the old bar. — [AIR Media-Tech timeline](https://air.io/en/monetization/youtube-monetization-policy-changes-2026-a-complete-dated-timeline)
- [SECONDARY] Since 31 March 2025 public Shorts view count counts any start/replay; "Engaged Views" keep the old stricter methodology (stay past opening seconds, exclude loops) and are reported as what counts for YPP/payouts (definitions vary by source). — [VidIQ via search summary](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/) (page returned 403; summary from search), [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-rate)
- [SECONDARY] "Viewed vs swiped away" (YouTube term "Stayed to watch") is the share who stay on a Short vs scroll past; creator sources describe it as the early test-audience gate; YouTube has not published a formula. — [Creator Essentials glossary](https://www.creatoressentials.com/glossary/viewed-vs-swiped-away/)
- [SECONDARY] Shorts RPM by country (AIR 2025 data, page updated 23 Jan 2026), USD per 1,000 views: US 0.328, Switzerland 0.205, Australia 0.193, South Korea 0.185, UK 0.166, Canada 0.165, Germany 0.163, Japan 0.144, Austria 0.135, France 0.102, Brazil 0.045, Mexico 0.040. ITALY NOT LISTED. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/monetization/what-rpm-can-you-expect-from-shorts-in-2026)
- [SECONDARY] General Shorts RPM after YouTube cut: most creators 0.03-0.10 USD; finance/tech higher, broad entertainment lower. — [VidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/)
- Italy long-form AdSense RPM approx. 2.52 USD (not Shorts, different revenue pool). — [Dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/it)

### Inferences
- A stock-footage + subtitle + TTS template at high upload pace matches the named exclusion traits; to stay safe, each video needs distinct script/angle, ideally a human voice and transformed footage. The policy text allows series with distinct concepts, so recurring formats are fine only if content varies materially.
- Italy Shorts RPM likely sits near Germany/France-level or lower (approx. 0.05-0.15 USD), but this is my estimate, not sourced. At such RPM, 1M Shorts views is roughly 50-150 USD; Shorts monetization is not the main income path for an Italian-language page.
- If the Feb 2027 thresholds are confirmed, getting monetized through Shorts alone becomes harder (20M views/90 days for new applicants).

### Gaps
- No Italy-specific Shorts RPM found.
- The 2026 YPP overhaul and January 2026 wave are single-source (AIR); no official YouTube blog fetched.
- YouTube's own documentation of Shorts ranking signals (swipe-away, loops) not retrieved.

## TikTok: Creator Rewards (Italy), unoriginal content, AI labeling, For You signals

### Takeaway
Italy is reported as eligible for the Creator Rewards Program (10k followers, 100k views/30 days, personal account, original content, videos over 1 minute), but this rests on third-party pages, not TikTok's own list; verify in app. AI-generated realistic content requires labeling.

### Cited Findings
- [SECONDARY, dated 9 July 2026] Program "confirmed in US, UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain"; requirements: 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in last 30 days, personal account in good standing (no business/government/political), original content, only videos longer than one minute earn rewards, legal residence in eligible country with local payout method. Page itself says to confirm in app (Profile → Menu → Creator Tools / TikTok Studio → Monetization). — [TTCalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-fund-countries/)
- [SECONDARY] Other guides repeat the same thresholds and list Italy under Western Europe; one claims 100% original content required (excluding duets, stitches, photo mode) and approval in days to weeks. — [Socialrevver](https://socialrevver.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program), [Multilogin](https://multilogin.com/blog/mobile/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/)
- [SECONDARY] AI labeling: realistic AI scenes/people must carry the "AI-generated content" label; TikTok also auto-detects via C2PA content credentials; minor edits and generic TTS reportedly exempt. Effective-date claims (rule version effective 24 Sept 2026) unverified. — [Sirency](https://www.sirency.com/blog/tiktok-ai-generated-content-label), [Pixo](https://pixo.video/blog/tiktok-ai-label-compliance-guide)
- [SECONDARY] Nov 2025: TikTok added a Manage Topics control letting users reduce AI-generated content in For You. — [TechEconomy](https://www.techeconomy.ng/tiktok-ai-content-control-feature)
- Claims that Creator Rewards explicitly bans AI content: unverified, conflicting framing ("minimal original input").

### Inferences
- A stock-footage page can plausibly qualify on paper, but "original content" is assessed by TikTok; compilations of third-party stock with TTS are at risk of being judged unoriginal. Videos must exceed 60 seconds to earn, which conflicts with short loop-style formats.

### Gaps
- TikTok's official eligible-country list, official unoriginal-content wording, and official For You signal list (completion, rewatch, shares, saves) could not be retrieved (support pages redirected/empty). These signals are commonly cited by creator guides but I have no primary citation.

## Instagram / Meta: originality, Reels signals, Facebook Content Monetization in Italy

### Takeaway
Meta's stated direction since 2022 and formalized July 2025 is demoting reposted/unoriginal content; Mosseri names watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as top Reels signals (sends weighted for non-follower reach). Italy availability of Facebook Content Monetization is only third-party-attested.

### Cited Findings
- [SECONDARY] Mosseri: watch time, likes per reach, shares (sends) per reach are key; rates matter more than counts; sends matter most for reaching non-followers. — [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm/) (via search summary)
- [SECONDARY] 2022: Mosseri said original creators should get more credit than resharers. — [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/instagram-reforca-combate-a-reposts-do-tiktok-e-de-outros-videos-no-reels/)
- [SECONDARY] July 2025 Facebook policy: accounts repeatedly reusing others' videos/photos/text lose monetization access and get reduced distribution; duplicates demoted; commentary/reaction/new perspective not penalized; ~10M impersonator profiles removed and ~500K spam accounts actioned. — [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/07/223-meta-cracks-down-on-unoriginal-facebook-content/), [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/facebook-push-against-unoriginal-content/), [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/facebook-penalise-accounts-sharing-unoriginal-or-reused-content-9497088)
- [SECONDARY, single source] Since 30 April 2026 Instagram originality enforcement extends to photos and carousels; accounts posting mostly unoriginal content excluded from recommendation surfaces (Explore, suggested); explicit topic preferences now shape feed distribution. Not confirmed from Meta. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/it/instagram-marketing/how-instagram-algorithm-works) (via search summary)
- [SECONDARY] Mosseri year-end 2025 post: bar moves from "can you create" to "can you make something only you could create"; plans to label AI content and rank for originality. — [Famous Campaigns](https://www.famouscampaigns.com/2026/01/10-hard-truths-about-instagram-in-2026/), [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/instagram-chief-warns-ai-generated-content-will-flood-feeds-flags-fingerprinting-of-real-media-86899.htm)
- Facebook Content Monetization Italy: one 2026 guide lists Italy among participating countries; a 2024 Performance bonus expansion named Italy, France, Germany, Spain; Meta's official list not found. Thresholds conflict across sources (e.g. 10k followers + 600k minutes/60 days vs 1k-10k followers). — [ShortSync](https://www.shortsync.app/resources/facebook-content-monetization-program-2026), [Sirency](https://www.sirency.com/blog/facebook-content-monetization), [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/facebook-launches-unified-content-monetization-program-for-creator-earnings/)
- Claims of "75% of US recommendations go to original content" and "60-80% reach drop for aggregators" are unverified; discount.

### Inferences
- Stock-footage + subtitles is not "reposted" in Meta's sense if the script and edit are original, but templated sameness across accounts/posts risks duplicate detection. Cross-posting the same file with a TikTok watermark is explicitly penalized.

### Gaps
- No primary Meta page retrieved; no confirmed Italy eligibility or Reels/Facebook payout rates for Italy.

## What works in 2026 for faceless creators

### Takeaway
Sources (mostly vendors) agree on: a niche with a named audience/angle, recurring single-concept series, a hook inside 2-3 seconds, burned-in captions (muted viewing), and retention over volume. Evidence is weak and often unsourced.

### Cited Findings
- Faceless still works but bar is higher; generic motivation / celebrity facts / AI-voice listicles most saturated; generic motivation invisible without a demographic angle. — [Kineclip](https://kineclip.com/blog/do-faceless-channels-still-work-2026/), [Shortsfast](https://shortsfast.com/blog/saturated-faceless-youtube-niches-2026/), [Faceless.my](https://faceless.my/niches/top-faceless-youtube-niches/)
- Swipe decision at about 2-3 seconds; on-screen captions function as the hook for muted viewers. — [Virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/first-3-seconds-hook-faceless-shorts-2026), [Kineclip hooks](https://kineclip.com/blog/how-to-write-viral-hooks-short-form-2026/)
- Targets quoted: 70%+ retention for Shorts under 60s (Virvid); 19% retention gain and 44% growth stats are secondhand Zebracat figures, unverified. — [Virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/faceless-youtube-algorithm-retention-2026)
- Episodic single-concept series recommended; growth typically after 50-100 videos over 6-12 months. — [Faceless.my](https://faceless.my/niches/top-faceless-youtube-niches/), [FlowShorts](https://flowshorts.app/blog/best-video-ideas), [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/faceless-youtube-channels/)
- Study outlier videos before choosing topics; test multiple hooks on same idea; Trial Reels (Instagram) reported as hook-testing tool (unconfirmed by Meta). — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/faceless-youtube-channels/), [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/is-instagram-penalizing-polished-content-in-2026-yes-heres-what-changed)

### Inferences
- Native/human voiceover and original scripts are the cheapest differentiators from the template traits YouTube lists; TTS + stock loops is the profile of the January 2026 enforcement (per AIR). I found no controlled data comparing AI visuals vs stock footage, TTS vs human voice, posting frequency, or loops vs non-loops.

### Gaps
- No independent, methodologically sound dataset on these tactics; loops, native voiceover vs TTS, cross-posting effects, and posting frequency lack primary evidence.

## Why generic niche pages stagnate and how to diagnose

### Takeaway
Plausible mechanism: recommenders test each video on a small audience inferred from topic/viewer interest; broad or inconsistent topics give a fuzzy seed audience. This is mostly creator-community theory rather than documented platform behavior.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram: explicit topic preferences plus behavior shape distribution; make niche obvious via captions, visuals, audio. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/it/instagram-marketing/how-instagram-algorithm-works) (single source)
- Diagnostic metrics named in sources: YouTube Studio "Viewed vs swiped away", average view duration / % viewed, traffic sources and impressions; Instagram sends per reach and watch time; check Account Status instead of shadowban tests. — [Creator Essentials](https://www.creatoressentials.com/glossary/viewed-vs-swiped-away/), [Blckalpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/what-is-a-shadowban-myths-and-evidence), [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm/)

### Inferences
- Suggested diagnostic set (my synthesis, not sourced as a standard): swipe-away/stayed-to-watch rate, average % viewed, shares (sends) per view, follower-to-view ratio, non-follower reach share, and per-topic spread of views to detect whether one sub-topic outperforms.

### Gaps
- No primary or data-backed benchmark values for these diagnostics; "topic classification and audience seed" is not documented by any platform in what I retrieved.

## Shadowban myths vs reality, warm-up and niche seeding

### Takeaway
"Shadowban" is not an official term, but reduced recommendation exists (Instagram "not eligible to be recommended", TikTok "FYF ineligible"). Warm-up schedules and "tier pool" thresholds are folklore.

### Cited Findings
- Mechanism real, term disputed; Mosseri has denied shadowbanning in the conspiracy sense while acknowledging reach limits for violations; apparent shadowbans are usually normal variance, documented recommendability limits, or rule violations. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/shadowbanned/), [Blckalpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/what-is-a-shadowban-myths-and-evidence), [MakeInfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/is-there-really-an-instagram-or-tiktok-shadowban)
- Warm-up (5-7 days of niche scrolling/engagement before posting) is widely recommended but not in platform documentation; TikTok's tiered test-pool is called folklore by one analysis while others state it as fact. — [Blckalpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/what-is-a-shadowban-myths-and-evidence), [SocialKit](https://socialk.it/en/blog/tiktok-account-warmup-guide), [Superscale](https://superscale.ai/learn/the-right-way-to-warm-up-new-tiktok-instagram-accounts/)

### Inferences
- Low-cost niche-consistent engagement before posting is harmless; avoid automation or engagement pods, which violate platform rules. Review Account Status / eligibility pages before concluding a ban.

### Gaps
- No YouTube-specific shadowban evidence; no controlled test of warm-up effect.
