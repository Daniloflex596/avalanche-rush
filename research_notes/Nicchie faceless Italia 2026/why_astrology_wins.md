# Why astrology/oroscopo/spirituality outperforms on short-form (Italy focus) vs motivational, psychology and curiosita faceless pages (as of 2026-10-07)

Method note: about 12 searches/fetches. Many structural claims (retention, share mechanics, monetization of specific sub-niches) have NO primary data found; they are marked as Inferences. Italy-specific quantitative data is thin and old.

## 1. Audience size, growth, demographics, Italian interest

### Takeaway
Astrology has a large, durable, female- and young-skewing audience (Pew 2025: 43% of US women 18-49 believe; ~3 in 10 US adults consult astrology/tarot/fortune tellers yearly). In Italy, oroscopo reading is mass-market (majority read or half-believe) but I found only dated surveys and no current search-volume or follower data.

### Cited Findings
- Pew (May 2025, n=9,593 US adults): about 3 in 10 Americans use astrology, tarot or fortunetellers at least once a year — [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/05/21/3-in-10-americans-consult-astrology-tarot-cards-or-fortune-tellers/)
- Belief in astrology 27% of US adults (29% in 2017, so stable, not a fad); women 35% vs men 18%; women 18-49 at 43%, men 18-49 20%; LGBT adults 43% vs 26% — Pew via secondary write-up [Bangor Daily News](https://www.bangordailynews.com/2025/05/21/nation/how-many-americans-believe-in-astrology-and-rely-on-fortunetelling-a-new-survey-has-answers/) (original: Pew link above)
- Italy: a survey roughly a decade old (2017 AGI) says a bit over half of Italians give some credit to astrology; at least two-thirds read the horoscope regularly; many say they do not believe but read anyway, even daily; interest peaks at year end — [AGI](https://www.agi.it/cronaca/news/2017-11-10/oroscopo_italiani_astrologia-2613611/news/), [Focus](https://www.focus.it/scienza/salute/natale-piu-di-1-italiano-su-2-crede-negli-oroscopi-picco-a-fine-anno)
- Doxa figure cited by INAF: trust in horoscope falls with education (13% graduates vs 20% diploma holders; a 64% figure for lowest education looks unverified) — [INAF](https://www.media.inaf.it/2012/06/01/non-e-vero-ma-ci-credo/)
- Italian incumbents: Paolo Fox called by far the most followed astrologer in Italy (Rai 2 "I fatti vostri", daily radio show, near-monopoly on horoscope); Branko writes for Il Messaggero and Chi, annual calendar with Mondadori — [Il Post, 2026-01-01](https://www.ilpost.it/2026/01/01/paolo-fox-oroscopo/), [Fanpage](https://www.fanpage.it/spettacolo/interviste/branko-oroscopo-conosco-la-data-della-mia-morte-sono-puro-non-amo-da-40-anni-paolo-fox-non-lo-frequento/)
- Market size estimates vary ~30x by definition: ~$4.75B 2025 at 20.5% CAGR (Business Research Co.) / ~$3B 2024 to $9B 2030 (MarkNtel) / narrow horoscope-app-only $153M 2025 — [GII](https://www.gii.tw/report/tbrc1730783-astrology-app-global-market-report.html), [MarkNtel](https://www.marknteladvisors.com/press-release/astrology-app-market-size), [Apptunix](https://www.apptunix.com/blog/top-horoscope-apps/). Paid-report figures, methodology unverified; Co-Star revenue claims unverified.
- Search interest spiked during 2020 (birth chart and astrology five-year peaks on Google Trends) alongside TikTok growth — [SW Londoner](https://swlondoner.co.uk/?p=68412) (secondary)

### Inferences
- Pew shows belief is stable (27% in 2017 and 2025), so astrology is a long-lived demand base rather than a trend; the heavy concentration among women 18-49 matches the highest-engaging short-form audience (comment/tag/share behaviour).
- In Italy, "read but not believe" means casual daily consumption is socially acceptable, which suits low-commitment short video.

### Gaps
- No current Italian Google Trends / search-volume number for "oroscopo" retrieved; no follower counts for Branko/Paolo Fox; no recent Italian survey (Doxa/Ipsos/Eurispes); no verified Italian gender/age split.

## 2. Retention, share, save, comment mechanics and what the algorithm rewards

### Takeaway
Instagram's stated top signals are watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach (sends matter more for reaching non-followers). Astrology's sign-based structure naturally generates sends/tags and comments, and its recurring format yields return viewers; however, no platform study directly measures this.

### Cited Findings
- Mosseri (2025): top ranking signals are watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach; likes slightly more important for connected audiences, sends for unconnected — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/). The "sends = 3-5x likes" multiplier is from third-party data and unverified — [eClincher](https://www.eclincher.com/articles/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works-in-2026).
- Barnum/Forer effect: people rate generic descriptions as personally accurate; a 1989 study found even skeptics rated astrological-labelled descriptions as more accurate than identical non-astrological ones — [SAGE (abstract)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0146167289154010), [Decision Lab](https://thedecisionlab.com/es-ES/biases/barnum-effect)
- Sign-based content is easy to format as memeable short video (rankings of signs, "tag a Leo"); birth-month trends show users commenting and sending to friends when results are unflattering — [SW Londoner](https://swlondoner.co.uk/?p=68412), [Daily Targum](https://dailytargum.com/article/2024/03/skeptical-about-astrology-try-asking-someone-their-birth-month) (journalistic, not studies)
- French reporting on tarot on TikTok: "pick a card" format opens with a question, viewers choose, which drives engagement leading to paid readings — [RTBF](https://www.rtbf.be/article/tiktok-le-nouvel-eldorado-des-voyants-10956118)
- Starcrossed app grew mainly from founder-led astrology TikToks, top video >130M views — [PlayKit newsletter](https://playkit.substack.com/p/starcrossed-astrology-app) (newsletter, single source)

### Inferences
- Mechanism stack (inferred, not directly sourced): (1) identity hook: 12 segments each personally relevant, so each video has 12 potential "this is me" moments; (2) forced participation: "tag your sign / comment your sign" yields comments and sends per reach; (3) rewatch: viewers wait for their own sign and often re-watch or watch all 12; (4) recurrence: daily/weekly/monthly forecasts create habitual return viewers and reusable series; (5) unfalsifiable/Barnum content is never "wrong", so credibility risk is low.
- Because sends per reach is a stated top signal for reaching new people, sign-tagging prompts are structurally aligned with the algorithm; curiosita and quote content has no named person to send to.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on astrology retention/share rates versus other niches; no TikTok- or Reels-specific benchmark comparing niches; no Italian creator case data.

## 3. Why motivational and psychology faceless pages underperform now

### Takeaway
They are crowded, undifferentiated, and the platforms now explicitly demote mass-produced/reposted content (YouTube inauthentic content, Instagram aggregator demotion). Psychology that does work is narrow, relationship-focused and identity-labelling (attachment styles, narcissism), but it carries credibility risk.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube, July 15 2025: "repetitious content" renamed "inauthentic content"; covers templated content with little variation or easily replicable at scale; applies to whole channel (monetization can be removed channel-wide); test: can the average viewer tell videos differ; reused content with significant original commentary is still allowed; YouTube says it is a clarification, not a new rule — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en), [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/)
- Instagram: aggregator accounts (10+ unoriginal posts in 30 days) removed from recommendations (2024), still reach followers; extended to photos and carousels on 2026-04-30; watermark, speed change, screenshot-with-credit do not count as original; commentary/humour/creative edits can — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/), [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)
- Vendor blogs say motivation and psychology are the most saturated faceless niches, generic AI output suppressed; advice is to niche down — [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/roundups/best-faceless-ai-content-niches-2026), [VidRush](https://vidrush.ai/blog/how-to-start-a-faceless-youtube-channel-beginner-guide). Low reliability (vendors selling tools); the VidRush claim of January 2026 YouTube channel removals is unconfirmed.
- Attachment-style content: #attachmentstyles 91.7M and #attachmenttheory 142.4M views (2022); critics note oversimplified self/partner diagnosis — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2022/08/20/why-attachment-theory-is-trending-according-to-dr-amir-levine.html), [i-D](https://i-d.co/article/attachment-styles-have-us-all-in-a-chokehold)
- NarcTok: #NarcTok 1.9B views; creators report 70-80% of followers are women aged 30-50; clinicians criticise engagement mistaken for expertise — [MindSite News](https://mindsitenews.org/2022/03/20/whats-behind-the-narcissism-obsession-on-tiktok-and-what-does-it-says-about-us/)

### Inferences
- Motivational quotes: interchangeable content, no audience segmentation, no reason to return or tag a specific person, easy to clone, so the most exposed to anti-aggregator/anti-template rules.
- Generic psychology facts: abstract, no personal identity hook, credibility attacks; the sub-niches that work (attachment, narcissist recovery, relationship dynamics) share astrology's traits: self-recognition ("this is me/my ex"), a female 25-50 audience, comment-heavy, sendable to a partner or friend.
- Curiosita/fun facts: commodity content, one-off consumption, no identity or series reason to follow, so views without follows (inferred; no source on curiosita pages specifically).
- Stoicism for men as a working sub-niche is user-suggested; I found no source validating it.

### Gaps
- No current (2026) data on growth of dark-psychology/stoicism pages; NarcTok/attachment data are 2022; no Italian-language evidence; nothing specific on curiosita pages; TikTok's own 2025-26 originality policies not retrieved.

## 4. Monetizable sub-niches and formats within astrology

### Takeaway
Astrology monetizes through direct paid consultation, apps, and physical goods far more than through platform payouts; Astrotalk shows the scale of the paid-consultation model (India).

### Cited Findings
- Astrotalk (India) FY25 operating revenue INR 1,176 Cr vs 651 Cr prior year; e-commerce arm (gemstones, rudraksha, jewellery) claims >INR 140 Cr in first 12 months; IPO planned 2027 — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/ipo-bound-astrotalk-finds-new-revenue-driver-in-ecommerce/) (single press source, unaudited)
- Creator astrologer revenue: brand partnerships plus link-in-bio booking of readings (Cole Prots) — [Snipfeed](https://snipfeed.co/blog/how-astrologer-cole-prots-is-taking-over-tiktok-one-roast-at-a-time)
- CreatorDB estimate for an Italian tarot/astrology creator (~592K followers): $3.8K-9.4K per IG post (third-party estimate, not earnings) — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/ivanadicosmo/)
- Apps: Starcrossed grew via TikTok (see section 2); US online astrology market ~$1.365B 2025, mobile apps fastest-growing segment (Ken Research) — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/us-online-astrology-market.md)

### Inferences
- Likely ladder: free daily/weekly oroscopo (reach) -> pick-a-card tarot (engagement) -> paid reading / birth chart / app or WhatsApp-Telegram community -> affiliate (candles, crystals, tarot decks, apps). Only paid-reading and app/e-commerce links are sourced; WhatsApp/Telegram communities, affiliate, digital products in Italy are unsourced.
- Faceless constraint: tarot and chart readings usually sell on a personality; faceless pages more plausibly monetize via apps, affiliate, lead-gen to a reader, or digital products (inference).

### Gaps

## 5. Practical diagnosis checklist (synthesised; not separately sourced)

### Takeaway
A page works when it has a segmented identity hook, a recurring slot, a built-in reason to tag/send, and a differentiated voice; curiosita/motivational/psychology pages usually lack these.

### Cited Findings
- See sections 2 and 3 for sourced platform rules (sends/watch time; Instagram originality; YouTube inauthentic content).

### Inferences
Checklist for a creator whose astrology page works and others do not:
1. Identity hook: does each video address a defined group (sign, relationship status) that can say "this is me"? Curiosita/motivation usually address everyone, so no one.
2. Named recipient: is there a natural person to tag or send to? (Sign-matching friends, partner, ex.)
3. Recurrence: is there a fixed slot (daily/weekly/monthly) giving reason to return and follow?
4. Comment trigger: explicit question ("che segno sei?") rather than passive consumption.
5. Rewatch/loop: do viewers need to wait for or hunt their segment?
6. Differentiation: is the voice, visual style or angle distinctive, or interchangeable template/AI/reposted material (exposed to YouTube inauthentic and Instagram aggregator rules)?
7. Credibility exposure: astrology claims are not falsifiable; factual/psychology claims are and invite pushback.
8. Monetization path: is there a next step beyond ads (reading, app, community, product)?
9. If switching, test psychology via segmented relationship formats (attachment style, narcissist-recovery, zodiac x attachment crossovers) rather than generic facts; test by sends/comment per reach, not views.
10. Italy: use Italian timing hooks (weekly oroscopo, year-end peak, Paolo Fox/Branko comparison content) with care for originality.

### Gaps
- None of the checklist is validated by experiments or creator Reddit threads; I did not find usable Reddit sources in the time available.
